================================================================
  ROLE PERMISSIONS
================================================================

  ADMINISTRATOR
  -------------
  - Full access to all modules
  - Manage Products (add, edit, delete)
  - Manage Stock levels
  - Manage Users (create, edit, delete)
  - View & clear Activity Logs
  - View & clear Login Attempt Logs
  - Access Maintenance (Categories)
  - View Sales Reports (all users)
  - Process POS transactions

  CASHIER (Staff)
  ---------------
  - Process POS transactions
  - View own Sales Reports
  - Access Dashboard (limited view)

================================================================
  HOW TO LOG IN
================================================================

  1. Open your browser and go to:
     http://localhost/jezz%20bakery%20managment%20system/

  2. On the login page, click the role tab:
     - "Cashier"       → pre-fills cblake / cblake
     - "Administrator" → pre-fills admin (enter password manually)

  3. Click "Let's Go!" to sign in.

================================================================
  DEVELOPER NOTES
================================================================

  - Passwords are stored as MD5 hashes in the database.
  - Activity logs are recorded for every user action.
  - Login attempts (success & failed) are tracked in login_log.
  - Product codes are auto-generated based on category prefix.
  - Product images are loaded via URL (paste any image link).
